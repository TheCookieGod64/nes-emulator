NESTOPIA EMULATOR CORE NOTICE & CREDITS
========================================

This project embeds and integrates the Nestopia NES/Famicom Emulator Core.

Nestopia is an open-source, cycle-accurate Nintendo Entertainment System (NES) and Famicom emulator written in C++ by Martin Freij, maintained and extended by R. Belmont and the Libretro / Nestopia UE (Undead Edition) team.
